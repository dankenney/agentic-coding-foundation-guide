# Lesson candidate

**Title:** A specific behavior to repeat or avoid.

**Status:** candidate / tested / adopted / rejected / retired

**Trigger:** What observable situation calls for this guidance?

**Action:** What should someone do, and why?

**Evidence:** Reproduction, comparison, artifact, or public primary source. State what was actually tested; an attractive idea can remain a candidate.

**Limits:** Where does it fail, cost too much, or require a different approach?

**Privacy:** Public-safe / private. Use synthetic examples for shared guidance. Keep private provenance separate; do not publish private URLs or confidential details.

**Source and freshness:** Public source URL/version, checked date, and next review date. For a private lesson, record the source only in the private record.

**Decision:** Who owns the review? What changed in the guide, workflow, or checks? If rejected or retired, record the reason and any replacement.

Promotion to adopted requires evidence appropriate to the claim and a human editorial decision. See [team practice](../guide/team-practice.md).
