# Lesson candidate

**Title:** Name a specific behavior to repeat or avoid.

**Status:** candidate / tested / adopted / rejected / retired

**Trigger:** How would someone recognize a situation where this advice helps?

**Action:** What should someone do, and why?

**Evidence:** Include a reproduction, comparison, artifact, or public primary source. Say what you actually tested. If the idea still needs evidence, leave it as a candidate.

**Limits:** Where does this fail, cost too much, or call for a different approach?

**Privacy:** Safe to share publicly / private. Use made-up examples in shared guidance. Keep private sources separate, and leave private URLs and confidential details out of the published version.

**Source and freshness:** Give the public source URL/version, the date you checked it, and the next review date. Keep a private lesson's source in the private record only.

**Decision:** Who owns the review, and what changed in the guide, workflow, or checks? If the lesson was rejected or retired, record why and note any replacement.

Before adopting the lesson, get evidence that supports the claim and a human review decision. See [team practice](../guide/team-practice.md) for using lessons across a team.
