# Handoff

**Goal and current state:** What outcome is being pursued? What works now?

**Location:** Target project directory, branch/commit, relevant files, artifact paths, and effective backend/environment. Note uncommitted work and ownership; distinguish the reference guide from the project being changed.

**Decisions:** Choices that affect the next step, with reasons or links to decision records.

**Evidence:** Exact commands or interactions, actual results, and saved evidence. Distinguish executed checks from proposed checks. Include live/UAT results when applicable.

**Unresolved:** Reproduction steps, failures, assumptions, missing access, and checks not run.

**Next action:** One concrete step, its expected result, and the existing authority boundary.

**Recovery:** How to resume safely or undo the change.

Do not paste a whole transcript. Give the next reader enough to inspect the current files and reproduce the evidence. Remove private data before sharing. See [recovery](../guide/recovery.md).
