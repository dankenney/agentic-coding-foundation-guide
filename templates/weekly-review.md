# Weekly review packet

**Period / reviewer:**

**Coverage:** Sources checked, dates/versions, failed fetches, missing access, and stale inputs. An incomplete scan remains incomplete. No recommended changes is a valid outcome.

## Recommended edits: 0–3

Repeat this block only for changes worth a decision:

- **Problem and reader:** What is wrong, missing, or newly useful?
- **Proposal:** Exact page and proposed diff; include removals.
- **Provenance:** Public primary source, published/updated date if known, checked date. Separate documented behavior, research findings, and our recommendation.
- **Evidence and limits:** What was tested? What remains uncertain? Which platforms are affected?
- **Decision:** adopt / trial / watch / reject; reviewer and reason.

## Retirements

Outdated or redundant guidance, reason, replacement/redirect where useful, and proposed removal diff.

## Private intake

Record private candidates in a separate private system. Publish only independently checked, generalized guidance with public-safe examples and sources. Do not put private links in this packet if it will enter the repository.

## Human review and completion

The agent may prepare research and reviewable changes within its existing authorization. A maintainer accepts or rejects adoption and authorizes consequential publishing under the project's rules. A scheduled run does not authorize merge, publication, or external messages.

After acceptance, record the reviewed commit, actual checks, and next review dates. See [maintenance](../maintenance/README.md).
