# Weekly review packet

**Period / reviewer:**

**Coverage:** List the sources checked, dates/versions, failed fetches, missing access, and stale inputs. Be clear about what you couldn't review. You don't need to recommend a change every week.

## Recommended edits: 0–3

Use this block for each change you'd recommend discussing:

- **Problem and reader:** What's wrong, missing, or newly useful, and who does it affect?
- **Proposal:** Name the page and provide the proposed diff, including removals.
- **Provenance:** Link the public primary source and give its publication/update date, if known, and the date you checked it. Distinguish documented behavior, research findings, and our recommendation.
- **Evidence and limits:** Say what was tested, what remains uncertain, and which platforms are affected.
- **Editorial review:** Record complete or pending for the natural writing pass, private voice pass, and final comparison with the intended change and sources. Name the pages reviewed and any unfinished work. Keep private skill details out of the packet; an unfinished pass means the edit is pending.
- **Decision:** Record adopt / trial / watch / reject, the reviewer, and the reason.

## Retirements

List outdated or redundant guidance, explain why it should go, and provide the removal diff. Include a replacement or redirect where useful. Use the same editorial review record for removals and replacement wording.

## Private intake

Keep private candidates in a separate private system. Before publishing a lesson from private work, check the guidance independently and use examples and sources that are safe to share. Leave private links out of any packet that will enter the repository.

## Human review and completion

An agent can prepare research and proposed changes within its existing authorization. A maintainer decides what to adopt and authorizes consequential publication under the project's rules. A scheduled run doesn't authorize a merge, publication, or external messages.

After acceptance, record the reviewed commit, the checks actually run, and the next review dates. Follow the [maintenance process](../maintenance/README.md) to apply the agreed changes.
